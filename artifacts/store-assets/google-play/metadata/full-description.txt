Learn God’s Word. Play with purpose.

Menorah Bible Quiz brings Bible learning, personal progress, and friendly competition together in one premium quiz experience.

LEARN THROUGH SOLO QUIZZES
Explore a 10-level journey across the Old Testament, New Testament, life of Jesus, apostles, Bible characters, Psalms and Proverbs, faith and prayer, and more.

DISCOVER SCRIPTURE AFTER EVERY ANSWER
See whether your answer was correct, review the correct choice, and continue learning through Scripture references and concise context.

COMPETE IN TWO WAYS
• Live Battle lets you create or join an online Bible quiz room.
• Friends Battle lets 2–4 players pass one device and compete together in person without a room code.

GROW OVER TIME
Earn XP, advance through levels, build your streak, review detailed results, and unlock achievements through real quiz activity.

LEARN IN YOUR LANGUAGE
Choose from multiple languages supported by the app. Question availability can vary by language and level.

Friends Battle is available for local shared-device play. Live Battle requires an internet connection.

Menorah Bible Quiz is designed for personal study, families, friends, youth groups, and anyone who wants to grow in Scripture through play.
