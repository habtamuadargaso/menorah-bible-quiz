# Quality Report

## Visual review

- Phone UI comes from real application routes and completed quiz flows.
- No browser chrome, localhost URL, developer tools, credentials, room codes, or admin content appears.
- Guest/empty Profile and Leaderboard states are retained rather than replaced with fabricated data.
- Captions use consistent top placement and do not cover controls.
- Approved navy, gold, cream, typography, logo, iconography, and hero artwork are used.
- Google feature graphic contains no ranking, rating, award, price, or unsupported claim.
- Daily Challenge and Church Mode are omitted from released-feature claims because their destinations are marked Coming Soon.

## Technical targets

- Apple iPhone: 1260 × 2736 opaque PNG, matching a current accepted 6.9-inch portrait class.
- Google phone: 1080 × 1920 opaque PNG, 9:16.
- Google feature graphic: 1024 × 500 opaque PNG.
- Apple and Google assets are stored separately.
- Tablet upload folders intentionally remain empty pending layout repair.

## Manual pre-upload checklist

- Reconfirm dimensions against App Store Connect and Play Console at upload time.
- Proofread metadata in the console because console normalization can alter line breaks.
- Confirm the privacy, support, and marketing URLs respond publicly.
- Review every screenshot at thumbnail size and full size.
- Obtain native-language approval before producing localized graphics.
- Record App Preview footage from a release build; the package currently contains a storyboard, not a fabricated video.
