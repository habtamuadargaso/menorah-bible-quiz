Learn God’s Word. Play with purpose.

Menorah Bible Quiz turns Bible study into an engaging quiz experience for individuals, friends, families, and faith communities.

STRENGTHEN YOUR BIBLE KNOWLEDGE
Work through a 10-level Solo Quiz journey covering the Old Testament, New Testament, life of Jesus, apostles, Bible characters, wisdom, prayer, and more.

LEARN AFTER EVERY ANSWER
Correct and incorrect answer feedback includes Scripture references and concise learning context, helping every question become a study moment.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Solo Quiz: build knowledge at your own pace
• Live Battle: create or join an online Bible quiz room
• Friends Battle: pass one device between 2–4 players and compete together

TRACK MEANINGFUL PROGRESS
Earn XP, advance through levels, build your streak, review results, and unlock achievements as you continue learning.

MULTILINGUAL SUPPORT
Choose from the languages available in the app. Question availability can vary by language and level.

Friends Battle is designed for in-person play on one device and does not require a room code. Live Battle requires an internet connection.

Menorah Bible Quiz is made for learning, friendly competition, and growing in God’s Word.
