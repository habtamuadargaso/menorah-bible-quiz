# Placement Guide

Measurements are starting recommendations for adult unisex sizes. Scale proportionally for youth, women’s fitted, and extended sizes after reviewing the vendor template.

| Product / location | Recommended artwork size | Placement |
|---|---:|---|
| Classic Hoodie front | 4.0 × 4.0 in | Centered, top of art 3.0–3.5 in below collar seam |
| Classic Hoodie back | 12 × 16 in | Centered, top of art 4.0–5.0 in below collar seam |
| Hoodie left sleeve | 2.5–3.0 in square | Centered on outer forearm, 3.0 in above cuff |
| Hoodie right sleeve | 3 × 11 in | Read from shoulder toward cuff, centered on outer sleeve |
| Premium T-shirt front | 3.5–4.0 in square | Center chest, 3.0 in below collar |
| Premium T-shirt back | 12 × 14 in | Centered, 4.0 in below collar |
| Polo left chest | 3.25–3.5 in wide | 7.0–9.0 in below shoulder seam, 4.0–6.0 in from center |
| Zip Hoodie left chest | 3.5 in wide | Minimum 1.5 in clearance from zipper |
| Zip Hoodie back | 12 × 16 in | Same as Classic Hoodie back |
| Baseball Cap | 2.15–2.35 in wide × max 2.1 in high | Centered on front panel; avoid center seam where possible |
| Coffee Mug | 9 × 3.75 in wrap | Center vertically; keep critical copy 0.25 in from wrap edges |
| Round Sticker | 6 in finished | 0.125 in bleed added by vendor; keep mark 0.15 in inside trim |
| Conference Banner | 8 ft × 40 in | Maintain 3 in physical safe margin; keep eyelets outside artwork |

Do not place the Menorah symbol across a garment seam, pocket, zipper, cap seam, or mug handle. Maintain clear space around the symbol equal to the width of its center stem.
